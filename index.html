How status is set: Available = scheduled CIT voice agents minus everyone off-phone in the interval (breaks, lunches, BOPs, training, other). Red is below the team minimum of 2 available. Amber is 2 up to the green level. Green is at or above the green level, so green intervals are not listed. A break is conflicting only when removing it would lift cover to a better level. This brief uses the published schedule, so borrowed or manual off-phone counts are not included.

Classification rules: Working agents are Present / On time when their first login is within 5 minutes after shift start, Tardy when later than 5 minutes, and Early when more than 60 minutes before shift start. An employee without a login is marked Absent only after the report cut-off reaches shift start + 5 minutes; otherwise they remain Not yet due. VL, SL, ML, other non-working schedule statuses, OFF, and missing schedule rows are listed separately. Name matching is exact after normalizing case and punctuation, with the existing username-to-employee-ID map taking priority. Review unmatched Intalk logins before treating the absent count as final.
